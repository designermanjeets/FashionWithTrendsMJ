Shipping & Delivery Policy

Last Updated: June 2026

At SURAJ WELLNESS PVT LTD (FashionWithTrends), we are committed to delivering your orders in a timely and secure manner. Please read our Shipping & Delivery Policy carefully before placing an order.

1. Order Processing

  • All orders are processed within 1–3 business days after successful payment confirmation.
  • Orders are not processed, shipped, or delivered on Sundays or public holidays.
  • During festive seasons, promotional events, or high order volumes, processing times may be extended.

2. Delivery Timeline

Standard Orders

  • Estimated delivery time: 7–15 business days from the date of order confirmation.

Bulk Orders

  • Estimated delivery time: 10–20 business days depending on quantity, customization requirements, and delivery location.

Please note that delivery timelines are estimates and may vary due to unforeseen circumstances such as weather conditions, courier delays, remote locations, or operational disruptions.

3. Shipping Coverage

We currently deliver across most locations in India through trusted courier and logistics partners.

For deliveries to remote or non-serviceable areas, additional delivery time may be required.

4. Shipping Charges

  • Shipping charges, if applicable, will be displayed at checkout before payment.
  • Any promotional free shipping offers will be clearly communicated on the website.
  • Additional charges may apply for bulk orders or special delivery requests.

5. Order Tracking

Once your order has been shipped, you will receive:

  • Shipment confirmation via Email and/or SMS.
  • Tracking details and courier information.
  • Real-time tracking updates through the courier partner's tracking portal.

If you do not receive tracking details within 5 business days of placing your order, please contact our support team.

6. Delivery Attempts

Our courier partners typically make multiple delivery attempts.

If delivery fails due to:

  • Incorrect address provided by the customer,
  • Customer unavailability,
  • Incorrect contact information,

the order may be returned to us. Additional shipping charges may apply for re-dispatching the order.

7. Delayed Deliveries

While we strive to deliver all orders within the estimated timeline, delays may occur due to:

  • Natural disasters
  • Weather disruptions
  • Government restrictions
  • Transportation issues
  • Courier partner delays
  • High seasonal demand

Fashion With Trends shall not be held liable for delays caused by circumstances beyond our reasonable control.

8. Damaged or Tampered Packages

Customers are advised to inspect the package upon delivery.

If you receive:

  • A damaged package,
  • An opened package,
  • Missing items,

please:

  1. Take clear photographs and videos of the package.
  2. Notify us within 24 hours of delivery.

Claims reported after this period may not be eligible for investigation.

9. Incorrect or Incomplete Address

Customers are responsible for providing accurate shipping information.

Fashion With Trends shall not be responsible for delivery failures resulting from incorrect shipping details provided during checkout.
