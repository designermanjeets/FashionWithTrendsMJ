Refund & Cancellation Policy

Last Updated: June 2026

At SURAJ WELLNESS PVT LTD (FashionWithTrends), customer satisfaction is our priority. This Refund & Cancellation Policy outlines the terms and conditions governing order cancellations, returns, replacements, and refunds for purchases made through our website.

1. Order Cancellation

Cancellation Before Shipment

Customers may request cancellation of an order before it has been shipped.

If the cancellation request is approved:

  • The order will be cancelled.
  • A full refund of the paid amount will be processed.

Cancellation After Shipment

Once an order has been shipped, it cannot be cancelled. Customers may refer to the Return & Refund section below for eligible return requests.

To request cancellation, please contact our support team with your Order ID.

2. Return Eligibility

Customers may request a return under the following circumstances:

  • Product received is damaged.
  • Product received is defective.
  • Wrong product delivered.
  • Product significantly differs from the description displayed on the website.

Return requests must be raised within 7 days of delivery.

To initiate a return request, customers must provide:

  • Order Number
  • Photographs of the product
  • Photographs of the packaging (if applicable)

FashionWithTrends reserves the right to inspect and verify the claim before approving the return.

3. Non-Returnable Items

The following products shall not be eligible for return or refund:

  • Products used, washed, altered, or damaged by the customer.
  • Products without original packaging, tags, labels, or accessories.
  • Products purchased during special clearance or final sale promotions.
  • Customized or made-to-order products.
  • Return requests submitted after the specified return period.

4. Refund Process

Once the returned product is received and inspected:

  • Customers will be notified regarding approval or rejection of the refund.
  • Approved refunds will be processed within 7–10 business days.
  • Refunds will be credited to the original payment method used during purchase.

Depending on the customer's bank or payment provider, the credit may take additional time to reflect in the account.

5. Replacement Policy

Where applicable, FashionWithTrends may offer a replacement instead of a refund.

Replacement requests may be approved for:

  • Damaged products.
  • Defective products.
  • Incorrect items delivered.

Replacement availability is subject to stock availability.

6. Return Shipping

If the return is approved due to:

  • Wrong product delivered,
  • Damaged product,
  • Defective product,

FashionWithTrends will arrange the return pickup or bear the return shipping cost.

If the return request is due to reasons not attributable to FashionWithTrends, return shipping charges may be borne by the customer.

7. Refund Exceptions

Refunds may not be issued in the following cases:

  • Delivery delays caused by courier partners.
  • Incorrect address or contact details provided by the customer.
  • Refusal to accept delivery.
  • Failure to respond to delivery attempts by the courier partner.
